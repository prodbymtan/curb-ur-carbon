# Curb Your Carbon

## About
- Curb Your Carbon was created by [Heona Liu](https://github.com/heonaliu) and [Tan Tran](https://github.com/prodbymtan) for Boston University's Hacks High School (BUHHS)'s Technology Innovation Scholars Program (TISP). Built (in 5 hours) on May 2nd 2026, winning 2nd place!

[Curb Your Carbon Presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1N7HMoFswfVzIVuNLFUIuw9IQsi1M9Tk9R_L4SwhKaSc/edit?usp=sharing)

## Why This Was Built
- The theme for this hackathon: Build a Greater Boston
- Boston and its cities are well known for its heavy traffic and heavy carbon emissions. Though drivers believe they make a free choice by driving, they don't realize their choice comes with a trade off of someone else's health. Boston's Roxbury, Dorchester, and much of South Boston has shown to have increasing asthma levels than the rest of Boston (which is why this issue is especially urgent).
- This project stemmed from Tan's childhood experience having to take precautions around the air quality, and Heona's past experiences when method of transportation (like taking the plane) caused the effects of carbon emissions to became apparent in her travels.


## Tech Stack
[Next.js](https://nextjs.org) framework, with Vite frontend, and Google Maps Platform API Intergration, and ML-based algorithm for Bluebikes
